Transcriber

Turns audio recordings into text and labels who is speaking. Everything runs on this computer, so recordings never leave it, and it works offline.

Recent transcripts

Drop an audio or video file hereor click to choose. MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, WEBM, MP4 and more

Speaker labels are a best guess. Click a name to rename it, or the dot beside a line to change who said it. Names you give are remembered, so the app can recognise those voices in future recordings.

Words with a wavy underline are ones the app wasn't sure about. Use "Next to check" to jump through them; click a word to fix it.
