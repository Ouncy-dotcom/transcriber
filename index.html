Transcriber

Turns audio recordings into text and labels who is speaking. Everything runs in your browser, so recordings never leave this computer.

Drop an audio or video file hereor click to choose. MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, WEBM, MP4 and more

Speaker labels are a best guess. Click a name to rename it, or the dot beside a line to change who said it. If you know how many people are talking, choosing that number above gives better results.
